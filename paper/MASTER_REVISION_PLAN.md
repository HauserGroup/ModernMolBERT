## Scope and decision rule

The most defensible near-term paper is a released SELFIES/ModernBERT encoder evaluated as a **frozen molecular representation**. The existing experiments do not isolate the effect of SELFIES, APE, and ModernBERT individually. Complete the required work packages below in preparation for **one full clean retrain of a corpus-only tokenizer and small encoder, followed by full re-embedding and downstream evaluation**. Fitting or refitting lightweight downstream heads is part of that evaluation. This replaces the previous zero-run assumption. The published preprint weights and results remain the historical comparator; the new run becomes the submission analysis only after coverage and evaluation checks pass. Do not add other encoder runs by default. A matched comparator is required only if the paper retains a causal methods claim.
